# Design System: The Letter

## Overview

The product is an authored archive of newsletter issues. It should feel like opening a dispatch index and laying one letter on a desk, not joining a marketing funnel. Search, section filtering, and selecting an issue are the complete loop; the reading sheet clearly says that no email is delivered.

## Colors

- **Paper** `#eee8dc`: warm desk ground.
- **Sheet** `#f8f4ea`: the selected issue surface.
- **Ink** `#18242b`: headlines and structural rules.
- **Editorial blue** `#35657a`: dek and reading emphasis.
- **Correction red** `#c84e3e`: issue numbers, section marks, and active controls.
- **Graphite line** `#bcb3a5`: filing rules and secondary structure.

Red is a filing/correction mark, not a decorative accent spread across every surface.

## Typography

Geist Sans provides the utilitarian archive labels and large opening statement. Geist Mono handles issue metadata, section tabs, and the delivery boundary. Georgia italic is reserved for the letter dek and body copy, giving the selected issue a reading voice distinct from the index.

## Layout

The first viewport moves from masthead and question-led hero into an asymmetric archive desk: issue index on the left, reading sheet on the right. On narrow screens the index precedes the sheet; search and section controls wrap naturally and the sheet keeps a comfortable reading measure.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is flat and editorial. Paper-to-sheet contrast, black rules, a tilted red stamp, and a short red filing bar create depth without shadows, gradients, or floating cards.

## Shapes

Panels are rectangular like paper and filing drawers. The stamp is the only rotated shape; its slight angle signals a physical archive mark. Tabs remain small rectangles with visible borders.

## Components

- **Issue index:** search, section tabs, numbered issue rows, and a no-results line.
- **Reading sheet:** issue metadata, filing mark, section, title, dek, authored body, and archive boundary.
- **Editorial states:** red active tab/number, blue reading voice, and a clearly labeled local archive.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do let the selected issue feel like a page being opened.
- Do keep the archive local and the delivery limit plain.
- Do use rules and typography to create hierarchy.
- Don't add a signup CTA, fake subscriber metrics, or an email-send promise.
- Don't turn each issue into a rounded SaaS card or use a generic gradient hero.
